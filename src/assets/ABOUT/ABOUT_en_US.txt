Description: Local Desktop Platform for Slope Stability and Retaining Wall Study
"Explore classical geotechnical and structural models — locally, visually, and deterministically."

GeoEstabil is a native desktop application engineered in Rust (Edition 2021) with an immediate-mode GUI powered by egui/eframe (GPU-accelerated rendering) and multi-core CPU parallelism via Rayon. Designed as an auxiliary tool for academic study, teaching, and computational experimentation, it integrates two main modules in a 100% local and offline experience:

1. Slope Stability (conceptual references ABNT NBR 11682:2009): Interactive 2D graphic editor for stratified profiles, phreatic water table, and surface surcharges, featuring vertical-slice equilibrium solvers (Fellenius, Simplified Bishop, and Simplified Janbu), parallel Rayon grid sweeps, FS Heatmaps, and critical circular surface highlighting.
2. Retaining Walls & Foundations (conceptual references ABNT NBR 6122:2022 and NBR 6118:2023): Preliminary simulation of gravity walls (cyclopean concrete and gabion) and reinforced concrete cantilever walls (stem, toe, and heel), covering Rankine and Coulomb earth pressures, external checks, and didactic rebar sizing, plus a local SQLite SPT soil catalog (NBR 6484 in `geoestabil.db`), headless CLI (`geoestabil-cli.exe`), and study report export (HTML5/PDF, Markdown, and SVG).

Commitment to Local Privacy (100% Local-First):
Your data never leaves your computer. GeoEstabil operates 100% offline, without cloud dependency, without telemetry collection, without user accounts, and without external AI or large language models (LLM/RAG).
