HIGHLIGHTS IN VERSION 2026.11.11.0 (INITIAL COMMERCIAL & OPEN-SOURCE RELEASE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. RAYON MULTI-THREADED SLOPE STABILITY SOLVER (ABNT NBR 11682:2009)
• Implemented Limit Equilibrium methods: Ordinary (Fellenius), Simplified Bishop (iterative m_alpha convergence with >= 0.20 numerical guard), and Simplified Janbu (shape factor f0).
• Parallel Rayon work-stealing grid sweeps across parameterized meshes (centers x_c, y_c and radii R), evaluating tens of thousands of candidate circular slip surfaces on CPU.
• Interactive 2D Canvas in egui/wgpu featuring automatic responsive resizing, cursor-centered Zoom, drag Pan, thermal Factor of Safety Heatmap overlay, and critical circle highlighting displaying vertical slices.
• Support for multi-layer stratigraphy, phreatic water table (u = gamma_w * h_w), distributed surface surcharge, and required Factors of Safety (High 1.50 / Medium 1.30 / Low 1.20).

2. INTEGRATED RETAINING WALL MODULE (ABNT NBR 6122:2022 AND NBR 6118:2023)
• Complete geometries: Gravity Wall (Cyclopean Concrete), Stepped Gabion Wall, and Reinforced Concrete Cantilever Wall (Stem + Toe + Heel).
• Active and Passive Earth Pressure theories: Rankine (horizontal and inclined backfill) and Coulomb (with wall-soil friction angle delta and inclined backface).
• External Geotechnical Stability Checks: Overturning (FS_overturning >= 1.50), Base Sliding (FS_sliding >= 1.50), Base Eccentricity (e <= B/6 middle-third kern) and maximum soil stress with trapezoidal or redistributed triangular diagram (2*N / (3*x_R)), plus Hansen / Meyerhof bearing capacity.
• Reinforced Concrete Structural Design (NBR 6118:2023): Partial safety factors gamma_f = 1.40, gamma_c = 1.40, gamma_s = 1.15, and CAA concrete cover; main flexural reinforcement plus minimum rebar (0.15%), ductility check x/d <= 0.45, unreinforced shear check (V_Rd1 >= V_d), and ELS-W crack-width check (w_k <= 0.30 mm).

3. BIBLIOGRAPHIC REFERENCES, MODELESS DIALOGS & MATHEMATICAL TYPOGRAPHY
• Independent Modeless Z-Order windows on Windows 11/10: Complete User Manual (F1 / Ctrl+M) with search, Markdown export, stacked fractions, subscripts, superscripts, and formatted tables (`doc_renderer.rs`); and 9-tab legal and technical About Dialog (Ctrl+I) with instant document search and formal bibliographic references to ABNT NBR 11682:2009, NBR 6122:2022, NBR 6118:2023, and allied standards under Third-Party Notices.
• Instant live runtime switching for language (Portuguese-BR / English-US) and high-contrast Light/Dark themes across every widget and dialog window.
• Local `.geoestabil` persistence, `%APPDATA%\GeoEstabil\geoestabil.db` SQLite database, HTML5/PDF, Markdown (`.md`), and SVG (`.svg`) reports, plus command-line utility (CLI) `geoestabil-cli.exe`.
