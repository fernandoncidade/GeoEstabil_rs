3. BIBLIOGRAPHIC REFERENCES OF TECHNICAL STANDARDS (ABNT) & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This application strictly adopts as methodological and terminological engineering references the Brazilian technical standards listed below (ABNT NBR 6023 citations), without reproducing their full texts, tables, or figures:

3.1. Primary Technical Standards Applicable to the Calculation Engine
• ASSOCIAÇÃO BRASILEIRA DE NORMAS TÉCNICAS. ABNT NBR 11682:2009 — Estabilidade de encostas (Slope stability). 2nd ed. Rio de Janeiro: ABNT, Aug 21, 2009 (effective Sep 21, 2009). 33 p. ISBN 978-85-07-01702-8. ICS 93.010. Referenced scope in project: minimum safety factor criteria (Table 1) and limit-equilibrium slope stability evaluation concepts.
• ASSOCIAÇÃO BRASILEIRA DE NORMAS TÉCNICAS. ABNT NBR 6122:2022 — Projeto e execução de fundações (Design and construction of foundations). 3rd ed. (ABNT NBR 6122:2019), incorporating Erratum 1 (Dec 15, 2021) and Amendment 1 (Mar 25, 2022). Rio de Janeiro: ABNT, 2022. 108 p. + 2 p. ICS 91.040; 93.010. Referenced scope in project: external geotechnical checks for shallow foundations and retaining walls (overturning, sliding, base eccentricity, and bearing capacity).
• ASSOCIAÇÃO BRASILEIRA DE NORMAS TÉCNICAS. ABNT NBR 6118:2023 — Projeto de estruturas de concreto (Design of concrete structures). 4th ed. (Aug 28, 2023; Corrected Version 2, Jan 30, 2024). Rio de Janeiro: ABNT, 2023/2024. 242 p. ISBN 978-85-07-09632-0. ICS 91.080.40. Referenced scope in project: partial safety factors, environmental aggressiveness classes, flexural design, unreinforced shear V_Rd1, and ELS-W crack width control.
